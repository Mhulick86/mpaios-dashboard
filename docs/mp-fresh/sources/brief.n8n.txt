Field-Specific Guidance
page_context
reader_persona: be specific. For a behavioral health agency page, this is likely a CEO, COO, or VP of Business Development at a treatment center evaluating agency partners. Name what they fear (low-quality leads, compliance violations, agency reps who don't understand the vertical) and what they need to feel (this team has been in my chair).
what_success_looks_like: one sentence tied to the CTA. Usually "Reader books a discovery call" or "Reader requests an audit."
search_intent: pick the actual dominant intent from the SERP for this keyword. Service pages are usually commercial.
on_page_seo
title_tag: under 60 chars. Include primary keyword. Include "Marketing Powered" only if it fits. Count characters and put the number in title_tag_length.
meta_description: under 155 chars. Lead with a specific number or credential. No clichés. Count characters.
h1: distinct from the title tag. Natural sentence containing the primary keyword. Operator voice.
primary_keyword.placement: standard placement rules — title, H1, meta, first 100 words, one H2, max 2 mentions in body.
secondary_keywords: 3-6 terms selected from the Ahrefs data. Prioritize volume above 100 with low KD. Each gets an explicit placement: "Use in H2 about compliance," "Work into FAQ #2," "Mention once in intro."
semantic_variants: 4-8 natural language alternates pulled from Ahrefs results and the task input. No placement rules.
outline
hook_direction: describe the opening angle, not the literal sentence. For MP pages, contrast with typical agency framing. The angle is usually "we have been in your seat" — operator credibility against agency outsider posturing.
h2_sections: 4-6 sections. Each one needs heading, what_to_cover (2-4 sentences of substance), target_words as a range, must_include (specific terms or facts the writer must hit), and proof_points (specific numbers or credentials to reference — can be empty for transitional sections).
closing_direction: what the reader should feel and do at the end. Tie back to the operator positioning and route to the CTA.
faq_section
4-6 questions. Pull from Ahrefs keywords that read as questions or can be reframed as questions (e.g. "marketing strategies for behavioral health" becomes "What marketing strategies work for behavioral health providers?"). Each gets 2-4 sentences of answer direction, not the literal answer.
internal_links
8-16 links. Hard cap: no more than 30% of anchor texts can be exact-match for the primary keyword. Mix exact, partial, semantic, and branded anchors. Each entry specifies which section the link belongs in. Pull all target_url values from the sitemap above.
cta
Primary CTA typically goes to /get-started/book-a-call/. For audit-focused pages use /get-started/request-an-audit/. Placement is usually after the second proof-heavy H2 and again at the end of the page.
voice_and_style
tone_direction: operator voice, direct, numbers over adjectives, no breathless consultant energy.
differentiators: 4-6 talking points the writer can weave in naturally.
proof_points_library: specific facts the writer can pull from. Always include: $50M+ managed in BH/MH, Haven 3→24 scale, court-certified expert witness, LegitScript and HIPAA awareness, AI-native since 2022. Add page-specific proof where it fits.
must_avoid: must include at minimum: em dashes, "crucial," "ever-evolving landscape," "landscape," "robust," "leverage," "unlock," "in today's fast-paced," "harness the power of," generic adjectives without numbers, vague consultant verbs.
compliance_guardrails
do_not_claim: outcome guarantees, cure language, specific success rates without a cited source, anything that reads as medical advice.
do_not_include: PHI, patient names, before/after of patients, identifying details of any individual in treatment, client logos that could imply a HIPAA covered entity relationship.
regulatory_notes: LegitScript certification context for addiction treatment ads, HIPAA awareness, Google Ads sensitive healthcare vertical means no retargeting, Meta Special Ad Categories restrict targeting on housing/health/credit-adjacent content.
Hard Rules

Every required field in the schema must be populated. No empty strings where content is expected.
Title tag and meta description must include accurate character counts in their respective length fields.
No em dashes anywhere in the brief output.
No clichés from the must_avoid list, even inside your own brief text.

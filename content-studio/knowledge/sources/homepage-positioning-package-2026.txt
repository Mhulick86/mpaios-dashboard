SECTION 05 / OWNED INFRASTRUCTURE / 177 WORDS


Infrastructure we own, operated for your business
Marketing Powered invests in and operates its own computing hardware and systems. Our owned
infrastructure gives us direct control over supported deployments, customization, and capacity instead of
relying entirely on rented access.
That lets us choose how a workflow runs and adapt the environment to its requirements. The value is
practical: more control over the systems supporting your marketing operation, not hardware for its own
sake.
Local processing
Supported workloads run on infrastructure we control, giving us direct oversight of data handling during
processing. Our local processing approach does not mean every integration runs offline or every external
service disappears. Clients retain ownership of their data; our ownership of the hardware does not change
that.
AI governance
Our AI governance approach combines access controls, testing before deployment, human review at
defined decision points, and clear accountability. We decide where automation is appropriate and where a
person must review the output or approve an action. Those controls support responsible execution without
promising that technology alone resolves every security or compliance obligation.
SECTION 06 / DATA SOVEREIGNTY, OPEN SOURCE, AND CUSTOMIZATION / 227 WORDS


Technology shaped around the way your business operates
The useful question is how technology improves control, fits your operation, and supports decisions your
team needs to make. Our approach addresses each concern directly.
Data sovereignty
Our data sovereignty approach makes storage, processing, access, and outside services explicit. We
define where information resides, how it moves, who can use it, and which providers participate in the
workflow.
You can see those choices rather than relying on a general assurance about privacy. The configuration
follows the engagement and approved data uses, including any third-party connections needed to operate
it.
Open source
Our open source approach uses adaptable software to select, configure, and integrate technology around
the business. We distinguish software under OSI-approved licenses from open-weight models released
under other terms.
Licensing and permitted use inform selection, not simply whether something is available to download. This
flexibility helps us avoid forcing an operational requirement into a product that was designed for a different
purpose.
Customizable AI
Our customizable AI work connects model configuration, approved business data, and workflows for
specific operational needs. Workflow integrations can support intake, attribution, reporting, or follow-up
without adding another disconnected task for your team.
We define the use case, available data, responsibilities, and limits before building. Clients retain their data
ownership; access to our services does not automatically transfer our proprietary technology, workflows,
or configurations.

tenure, not an agency result or a claim that marketing alone caused it.
Evaluate client results against the starting point, measurement period, work performed, and business
context. A useful result should help you make a decision, not simply give you a larger number.
